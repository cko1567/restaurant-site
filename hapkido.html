Program Details

Self-defense beyond sport rules.

Hapkido introduces students to a practical self-defense mindset. Training emphasizes awareness, movement, control, and the ability to respond with confidence.

Students learn concepts influenced by striking, grappling, balance, and leverage while maintaining the discipline and respect expected in martial arts training.
